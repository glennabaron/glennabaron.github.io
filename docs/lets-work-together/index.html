Let's Work Together # #

I am excited to announce that after taking some time away from work on sabbatical, I am refreshed and ready to start to looking for my next role!

I am looking for a role where I can build impactful products and help teams to do their best work. My most recent role was in engineering leadership, but I am also open to pivoting to a role where a technical background can make a big impact. That might translate to a job title of Technical Product Manager, Technical Program or Project Manager, or Engineering Manager! Having spent the last decade in tech, with five of those years in leadership roles, there are so many ways I can utilize my experience to make a difference on a team.

In my most recent role as Director of Engineering, I spent my time:

  • Leading multiple full stack engineering teams, providing technical leadership and direction
  • Directly managing and coaching Engineering Managers and all levels of individual contributors
  • Collaborating closely with Product, Data, Marketing, and Creative teams to define features and create a long term product strategy
  • Running scrum meetings, and working closely with Project Managers to build timelines and roadmaps
  • Collaborating with my peers in leadership to align and standardize on best practices

If you or someone you know has the inside scoop on a role you think I’d be a good fit for, please reach out via LinkedIn or email!

Who am I? # #

As someone with a background in both engineering and design, I’ve always loved figuring out how to make things, whether it’s a custom typeface or a CSS framework. I am passionate about connecting people, asking questions, and building. If your team shares those values, I'd love to work together!

Most recently, I’ve had the opportunity to lead engineering teams in varying industries (deregulated energy, telecommunications, government contracts), each with their own specialized areas of knowledge, unique technical requirements, and product goals. Earlier in my career, I worked at agencies building award winning digital experiences for local and national clients. Prior to that, you could find me tinkering with front end code and sharpening my graphic design skills.

Today, I value building stable, efficient teams with low attrition rates, and coaching managers and individual contributors to reach their full potential. I’ve shaped teams that can meet tight deadlines in fast-paced environments, balancing stability with speed, launching new features to millions of users. I work closely with each team member to help them achieve their career goals, while looking for opportunities to match their goals with those of the team. I build solid, trusting relationships with peers across product, creative, marketing and other engineering teams, helping bring roadmaps to life, and ensuring the successful delivery of high-quality features and products that delight users.
